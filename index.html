Problem & Solution Overview

Why UniFlow exists and how it transforms student information management.

1. The Problem

University students receive critical academic, career, social, and administrative information scattered across dozens of unintegrated channels—LMS portals, university web pages, email newsletters, WhatsApp group chats, club Instagram accounts, and physical bulletin boards.

This structural fragmentation creates acute information overload. Important scholarship deadlines are buried in mass emails, assignment updates are missed across disparate course portals, and career-building opportunities are lost simply because students lack a centralized, intelligent priority filter.

2. Mini Campus Research & Student Survey Data

📊 Student Field Survey

To validate our problem hypothesis, we conducted a field survey with university students. The survey confirmed significant fragmentation and administrative stress across student workflows:

100%

Multi-Platform Fatigue

Report regularly checking 2 to 4+ separate portals (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, official web portals).

38%

Missed Opportunities

Have missed key scholarships, internships, or academic competitions due to scattered updates.

88%

Information Overload

Feel overwhelmed tracking assignments, exam dates, and changing course deadlines manually.

Anonymous Student Survey Responses

"Finding important university information and missing out on scholarships and internships is my biggest frustration..."
"Keeping track of assignments, exams and deadlines across buggy websites is very overwhelming..."

3. The Solution: UniFlow

UniFlow acts as a unified intelligence engine for student life. Rather than adding another task manager, UniFlow aggregates fragmented updates into a single dashboard and applies user preference models to filter out irrelevant noise.

Core Experience Architecture: COLLECT → FILTER → PRIORITIZE → ACT

4. Why It Matters

Reduces Information Overload

Replaces dozens of scattered notification badges with a single dashboard showing what matters in seconds.

Prevents Missed Deadlines

Intelligent priority scoring surfaces urgent coursework and lab reports before they slip through the cracks.

Democratizes Opportunities

Surfaces high-value scholarships and internships directly matching field of study and interests.

Personalized Student Experience

Empowers students with continuous relevance feedback loops ("Relevant / Not relevant") to tailor their campus updates.
